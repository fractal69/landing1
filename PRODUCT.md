# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners in Colombia who are shopping for curtains, blinds, and window coverings — predominantly older adults who care about home decoration and value quality, trust, and personal service.

## Product Purpose

Cortinas Sergio helps Colombian homeowners select, purchase, and install curtains and blinds for their homes. Success means the visitor orders products and schedules installation.

## Positioning

The trusted local full-service window-covering provider in Colombia: shop quality curtains and blinds, then have them professionally measured and installed — no hassle, no mistakes.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on desktop or mobile looking for window-covering solutions. They may compare products, want guidance on sizing and materials, and need reassurance about quality and installation. Many are older, less digitally fluent, and value trust signals (local presence, phone contact, before/after photos). The purchase flow includes selecting products and booking installation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product catalog: curtains and blinds (types, fabrics, colors, sizes)
- Professional measurement and installation service
- Service area: Colombia
- Language: Spanish
- Physical showroom presence implied (trust factor for older buyers)
- No e-commerce platform built yet — this is a new build

## Brand Commitments

- Business name: **Cortinas Sergio**
- Operates in Colombia
- Spanish-language site

## Evidence on Hand

No existing site, testimonials, case studies, or imagery provided. Future work must not fabricate specific customer stories or claims without real evidence.

## Product Principles

1. **Trust is the conversion lever.** Older buyers need to feel confident before purchasing. Local presence, clear contact, and professional presentation earn that trust.
2. **Clarity over cleverness.** The audience skews older and less digitally fluent. Copy must be direct, readable, and helpful — no jargon, no gimmicks.
3. **Service is the differentiator.** The sale includes installation. The page must communicate that Cortinas Sergio handles everything from selection through professional setup.
4. **Mobile-first, but not mobile-only.** Many visitors browse on phones but may prefer desktop for purchase. Responsive design is essential.
5. **Show, don't just tell.** Product photography, fabric visuals, and real installation examples matter more than abstract claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Legible type sizes for older readers (minimum 16px body text)
- High contrast for readability
- Simple, clear navigation and form labels
- Touch-friendly tap targets (minimum 44px)
